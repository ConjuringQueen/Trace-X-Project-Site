STEP 01 · INPUT

Sensors are the motor’s senses.

A temperature sensor can notice heat. Other sensors may measure electrical current, voltage, speed, or vibration. Each sensor only tells us what it was built to measure.

The team confirms which sensors are connected and what units they use.
